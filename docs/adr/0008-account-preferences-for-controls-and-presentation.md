# Account Preferences For Controls And Presentation

Rune Lanes stores hotkey bindings and visual presentation choices as Account preferences, separate from Profile identity, Hero progression, and private Seat link access.

Account preferences are account-synced because controls and presentation are personal client choices that should follow a signed-in player across devices. Theme, motion preference, animation speed, board scale, and hotkeys can change how a player operates or views Rune Lanes, but they do not change match legality, match state, replay frames, hidden information visibility, or shared-match protocol semantics.

Profile remains the player-facing identity surface: display name, generated avatar, preferred Hero until the future Hero organization surface exists, progression summary, and owned history. Hero mastery, Skill trees, Rune loadouts, Deck recipes, and frozen match snapshots keep their existing ownership and replay boundaries.

Private Seat links remain capabilities. A Seat link is still sufficient to open and play that seat in a Shared match, whether or not the viewer is signed in. When a signed-in viewer opens a Seat link, their Account preferences may shape their local controls and presentation, but the Account does not become the authorization source for that Seat.
